About us

Three paddlers.
One crew.

Pistyll is Welsh for waterfall, which is usually where you'll find us: kayaks on our shoulders and cameras in our dry bags. We are Andy, John and Huw, three friends from the white water world who kept bringing a camera along. Because we are paddlers first, we can get to places most crews can't, keep the kit safe when we get there, and know the moment worth filming before it happens.

Andy Kettlewell

Co-founder

Andy has paddled since joining Viking Kayak Club in 2003, raced slalom inside the UK top ten and coached for Canoe Wales. He shoots from the water as often as the bank, usually with a camera on his helmet.

What we do

Photo & film,
wherever it happens.

Stills, films or both, shot and edited by the same small crew. We plan every job around where it is and what you need from it, whether that's a gorge in the Alps or a conference room in Cardiff.

01

Occasions & events

Races, festivals, launches and celebrations. We cover the whole day and turn round edits quickly so you can share while people are still talking about it.

02

Sports teams & clubs

Squad photos, match and race-day coverage, recruitment films and sponsor content, from people who have trained and competed in clubs themselves.

03

Individual athletes

Portfolio images, sponsor edits and expedition films. We paddle alongside you, so we're in the right spot when you land the line.

04

Corporate projects

Brand films, product and location photography, aerial work and social content for businesses that want to look as good as the places they work in.

Our passion

It all starts
on the river.

Kayaking is why Pistyll exists. Between us we have decades on rivers at home in Wales and away in the Alps, Mexico and Sumatra: racing, coaching, running expeditions and filming our friends on the hardest water they can find.

That time on the water is what makes us useful to you. We read rivers, we know rescue, and we can carry a camera into a gorge and bring it back out dry. The same instincts carry over to every brief, on or off the water.
